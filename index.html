Xinge Yang (杨辛格)

I am a Member of Technical Staff at OpenAI. I received my Ph.D. from KAUST, where I worked with Prof. Wolfgang Heidrich in the Computational Imaging Group. My Ph.D. research focused on two directions: (1) AI for optics, developing differentiable simulation and automated optimization to design optical systems, and (2) computational imaging, building computational cameras by co-designing optics with neural networks.

My Ph.D. research advanced differentiable optics by establishing a complete simulation framework covering ray tracing, coherent and polarization simulation, sequential and non-sequential optical models, and ray–wave optics conversion. My representative work, published in Nature Communications, proposes automated lens design via differentiable optimization. These efforts have been released as DeepLens, an open-source differentiable optical simulator integrating optics, sensors, and image processing for end-to-end imaging system design.

Research

My research develops AI methods for optics and uses them to build new imaging hardware:

  • AI for Optics: differentiable optical simulation, automated design algorithms, high-performance computing for optical simulation, and agentic workflows for optical design.
  • AI Imaging Hardware: next-generation cameras built by co-designing optics and algorithms, including end-to-end simulation of optics, sensor, and ISP, high-fidelity synthetic data that bridges the sim-to-real gap, and efficient on-device algorithms.
